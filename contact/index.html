What you can contact us about

We are dedicated to providing the most reliable automotive consumer resource in the UK. We encourage readers to reach out regarding:

  • Factual corrections: If you spot a legislative update, revised DVSA test fee, altered expiry regulation, or broken GOV.UK link on any page, please email us with the URL and relevant documentation so we can promptly update our guides.
  • Guide suggestions: If there is a specific MOT topic, technical failure reason, or used-car purchasing dilemma you would like our research team to investigate and explain, we welcome your topic suggestions.
  • Accessibility and usability: If you experience any technical difficulties reading our articles, navigating via keyboard or screen reader, or viewing diagrams on mobile devices, please let us know so our developers can make immediate accessibility adjustments.
  • General consumer feedback: We value feedback from UK vehicle owners, MOT testing technicians, and automotive specialists regarding their real-world inspection experiences.

Matters we cannot assist with

To avoid disappointment, please note that MOT History Check is an independent informational guide and not a government agency or licensed repair garage. We cannot assist with the following matters:

  1. Individual test appeals: We cannot overturn an MOT failure, modify an official test certificate, or adjudicate disputes between vehicle owners and testing stations. All formal test appeals must be filed directly with the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency (DVSA) using form VT17 within 14 working days of the test.
  2. Vehicle booking and garage appointments: We do not operate testing facilities or schedule garage appointments. To book an inspection, contact a local authorised MOT test centre directly.
  3. Vehicle registration and tax issues: We do not hold access to DVLA driver records, V5C logbook transfers, or vehicle tax payment portals. Visit gov.uk for all vehicle registration administration.
  4. Legal representation: While our guides quote relevant sections of the Road Traffic Act 1988, our team cannot provide formal legal advice in road traffic court prosecutions. Consult a qualified solicitor or legal advice service for court proceedings.
