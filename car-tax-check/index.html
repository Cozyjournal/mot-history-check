What the three tax statuses mean

Every UK vehicle sits in one of these three states. The check reads the current one straight from the official record.

Taxed

The vehicle is taxed up to the due date shown. It can be driven and parked on public roads, as long as it also has a valid MOT (if it needs one) and insurance.

Untaxed

The tax has run out and no SORN is in place. The vehicle must not be driven or kept on a public road. The keeper risks a fine, and the vehicle can be clamped or impounded.

SORN

The keeper has made a Statutory Off Road Notification. The vehicle must be kept off public roads entirely, on a driveway, in a garage or on private land, until it is taxed again, sold, scrapped or exported.

One journey is still allowed for untaxed and SORN vehicles: driving to a pre-booked MOT test. Our guides cover the rules in full, can you tax or insure a car without an MOT, and SORN and MOT rules.

Tax and MOT work together

You cannot tax a vehicle that needs an MOT but does not have a valid one, the tax service checks the MOT record before it lets you pay. That is why this result shows both together: if the tax is due soon and the MOT has expired, book the MOT first, then tax the vehicle as soon as it passes.

Buying a used car? Tax does not transfer with the vehicle, it ends when the car is sold, and the buyer must tax it before driving away. Check the status here before you view, then run the full MOT check to read its test history.

How much the tax will be depends on the vehicle's first registration date and CO2 emissions, and the rates change each April, GOV.UK shows the exact amount when you tax it. This check shows the status and due date, never a guessed price.

Questions about this check

Is this car tax check free?

Yes. The check is completely free and needs only the vehicle's registration number. No account, no logbook, no payment details.

What does the tax check show?

Whether the vehicle is taxed, untaxed or declared off the road (SORN), and the date the tax is next due. The same result also shows the vehicle's MOT status and expiry date, plus its make, model, colour and fuel type.

Does the check show how much tax I have to pay?

No. The check shows the tax status and due date, not the amount. How much you pay depends on when the vehicle was first registered and its CO2 emissions, and rates change each April. GOV.UK shows the exact amount when you tax the vehicle.

What happens if a vehicle is untaxed?

An untaxed vehicle must not be driven or parked on a public road unless it is being taken to a pre-booked MOT test. The registered keeper can be fined, and the vehicle can be clamped or impounded. Tax it online with the reference number from the V5C logbook or the reminder letter, or declare it SORN if it is kept off the road.
