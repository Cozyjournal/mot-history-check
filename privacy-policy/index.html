Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last Updated: October 2024 • Compliant with UK Data Protection Act 2018 & UK GDPR

1. Overview & Data Philosophy

At MOT History Check, we prioritize visitor privacy. As an informational guide, we do not collect or store personal vehicle data, driver information, or credit card details. We never operate user registration systems or maintain private vehicle tracking logs.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

When you enter a vehicle registration plate in our educational hero search box, that registration is used solely client-side to redirect you to our step-by-step guidance page and assist you with instructions for the official GOV.UK checker. We do not store, log, or sell vehicle registration numbers.

3. Cookies & Advertising Practices

This website may utilize standard HTTP cookies to enhance site functionality (such as remembering checklist choices or mobile navigation preferences). In the future, we may partner with reputable third-party advertising networks, such as Google AdSense, to serve non-intrusive contextual advertisements that support our free content.

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings (https://adssettings.google.com).

4. External Links to Third-Party Portals

Our guides link directly to external websites, including official government portals (gov.uk and nidirect.gov.uk). We are not responsible for the privacy policies or practices of external services. We encourage users to review the privacy notices of any external site they visit.

5. Contacting Our Data Controller

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights under the UK GDPR, please reach out through our Contact Page.
