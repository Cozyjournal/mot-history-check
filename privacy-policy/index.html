Privacy policy and data protection

Last reviewed: October 2026 • Published by MOT History Check

This privacy policy explains how MOT History Check handles your information when you visit our website or use our vehicle lookup tools. In short: we set zero tracking cookies, collect no personal marketing data, run no third-party analytics scripts, and do not store or log the vehicle registration numbers you check.

We believe online privacy should be the default standard rather than a complex opt-out settings panel. Because our service does not sell commercial advertising, profile user behaviour, or monetize personal information, our technical architecture is built from the ground up around strict data minimisation principles under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Zero cookies policy

Unlike most commercial automotive portals, MOT History Check sets absolutely no cookies on your device. We do not use:

  • Advertising or re-targeting cookies (such as Meta Pixel, Google Ads, or commercial ad networks).
  • User profiling or behavioral tracking cookies.
  • Third-party analytics cookies (such as Google Analytics or Hotjar).
  • Cross-site tracking identifiers or browser fingerprinting scripts.

Because we do not store any non-essential cookies or access local device storage for tracking purposes, you will not encounter an annoying cookie consent banner or privacy wall when visiting our site.

2. No browser localStorage or persistent client storage

Our client-side scripts do not store your search history, viewed number plates, or preference settings in your browser's localStorage, sessionStorage, or IndexedDB storage. Every time you close or refresh your browser tab, your browsing session leaves behind zero retained data footprints on our behalf.

3. How vehicle registration numbers are handled

When you enter a vehicle number plate into our search box on the homepage or guide pages, here is exactly what happens behind the scenes:

  • Lookup via official GOV.UK: While direct API proxy lookup is in development, submitting a registration plate simply copies the formatted text to your local clipboard and opens the official, secure GOV.UK testing portal in a separate tab. The number plate is never transmitted to or stored on any server owned by this project.
  • Lookup via Serverless Proxy (when active): When direct API lookup is configured via our Cloudflare Worker proxy, the plate string is sent over encrypted HTTPS solely to query the official DVSA MOT History API. The serverless proxy validates the plate format, retrieves the official test records, and returns the response immediately to your browser.
  • Zero logging of registration numbers: The lookup proxy explicitly does not record, log, or persist vehicle registration numbers in database records or server log files. Once the test response is delivered, the query string is discarded from temporary memory.

4. External requests: Google Fonts

To deliver a consistent, legible reading experience across modern mobile devices and desktop screens, our website loads three open-source font families (Sora, Figtree, and JetBrains Mono) directly from the Google Fonts content delivery network (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com).

When your web browser downloads these font stylesheet and font asset files, an automated HTTP request is made to Google's servers. Google receives standard network transmission metadata (including your device's IP address and user-agent string) solely to deliver the font files efficiently. No authentication cookies or user account credentials are exchanged with Google Fonts during this process.

5. Hosting and infrastructure (GitHub Pages)

This website is hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages (operated by GitHub, Inc., a subsidiary of Microsoft Corporation). When you visit any page on https://cozyjournal.github.io/mot-history-check/, GitHub's edge web servers may automatically log basic technical transmission headers, such as:

  • Your device's public IP address.
  • The date, exact time, and duration of the page request.
  • The specific requested file path (e.g., /mot-history-check/mot-cost/).
  • The referring URL and browser user-agent header.

GitHub processes these technical server logs to protect platform security, mitigate distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks, and maintain network reliability in compliance with GitHub's Global Privacy Statement.

6. Communications via email

If you contact our editorial desk by emailing [ADD EMAIL], your email address and any information contained within your message are utilized strictly to respond to your inquiry or review your editorial feedback. We never share, sell, or rent reader email addresses to commercial marketers or third-party mailing lists. Once an inquiry is resolved, correspondence is routinely deleted.

7. Your legal rights under UK GDPR

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018, UK residents possess statutory rights regarding personal data processing, including:

  • The right to be informed about how data is handled.
  • The right of access to any personal data held about you.
  • The right to rectification of inaccurate information.
  • The right to erasure of personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • The right to object to or restrict processing.

Because MOT History Check does not collect user accounts, names, residential addresses, or personal identity profiles, we generally hold no personal data linked to individual visitors. If you have questions concerning data protection, you may contact our privacy desk or reach out to the UK's independent supervisory authority, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
