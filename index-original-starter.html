What an MOT record shows

Every test is stored against the number plate. For cars, motorcycles and vans the online record goes back to 2005.

Pass or fail, and when

Each test date and result, plus the certificate expiry and when the next MOT is due.

Mileage at every test

The odometer reading recorded each year. A reading that drops is the quickest sign of clocking.

Failures and advisories

What failed, and what the tester flagged as worn or likely to fail soon.

Safety recalls

Depending on the manufacturer, the record can also show an open safety recall.

How defects are graded

AdvisoryWorth monitoring or repairing. Not a fail on its own.
MinorNot serious enough to fail the vehicle. Fix it soon.
MajorFails the test. Repair before the vehicle is driven again.
DangerousFails the test. Illegal to drive on public roads, even if the previous MOT has not expired.

Spot a clocked car in seconds

Read the record oldest test first. Mileage should only go up. Also watch for the same defect every year, and fails followed by a pass with no repair detail.

Mileage is 19,800 lower than the year before. Ask the seller to explain before going further.

An MOT only shows condition on the day of the test. Pair it with service history, a V5C check and a vehicle history check for finance, write-off and stolen markers.

Key MOT facts

First MOT3 years after first registration in England, Scotland and Wales. 4 years in Northern Ireland.
After thatEvery 12 months.
Testing earlyUp to a month (minus a day) before expiry keeps the same renewal date.
Maximum fee, car£54.85. Many test centres charge less.
No valid MOTFine of up to £1,000. Separately, driving a vehicle in a dangerous condition can mean up to £2,500 and 3 penalty points.
ExemptionsMost vehicles first registered over 40 years ago and not substantially changed.
Record coverageCars, motorcycles, vans: since 2005. HGVs, trailers, buses, coaches: since 2018. Northern Ireland: since 2017.

Questions people ask

Is checking MOT history free?

Yes. The official GOV.UK service is free and needs only the registration number. Be cautious of sites that charge for a basic MOT check.

How far back does MOT history go?

Cars, motorcycles and vans: tests since 2005. HGVs, trailers, buses and coaches: since 2018. Northern Ireland results start in 2017.

Do I need the V5C logbook?

Not for the results, which only need the number plate. To see where each test was done, GOV.UK asks for the 11-digit reference number from the V5C.

Can I get an MOT early without losing my renewal date?

Yes, up to a month (minus a day) before the current certificate expires.

What happens if my vehicle fails with a dangerous defect?

You must not drive it on public roads until it is repaired, even if the previous MOT certificate is still valid.
