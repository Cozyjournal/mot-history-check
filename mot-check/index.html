What this check shows

One plate gives you the vehicle's whole MOT story. Everything below comes straight from the official DVSA test record.

MOT status & expiry

Whether the MOT is valid today and the exact date it expires, so you know where you stand before driving or taxing the vehicle.

Full MOT history

Every test on record — pass and fail results with dates, going back to 2005 for cars, motorcycles and vans.

Mileage at every test

The odometer reading recorded at each test. A reading that goes down is the clearest warning sign of a clocked car.

Failures & advisories

What failed each test, and the advisories testers flagged as worn or on the way out — the best clue to future repair bills.

Tax status

Whether the vehicle is taxed, untaxed or declared SORN, and the date the tax is next due.

Vehicle details

Make, model, year, colour, fuel type and engine size, so you can confirm the plate matches the vehicle in front of you.

How to read the result

Start with the two status cards. A valid MOT with months left and a taxed status means the vehicle is legal to drive today. Then read the history from the oldest test to the newest: mileage should climb steadily, and the same advisory appearing year after year means a repair that keeps being put off.

A single fail is not a red flag on its own — most vehicles fail at some point, usually on cheap items like lamps, tyres and brakes. What matters is the pattern: repeated failures on the same parts, a mileage drop, or a long gap with no tests at all. Questions about this check

Is this MOT check free?

Yes. The check is completely free and needs only the vehicle's registration number. You do not need an account, the V5C logbook, or any payment details.

What does the MOT check show?

Whether the vehicle has a valid MOT and when it expires, every past MOT test result with its mileage reading, the failures and advisories recorded at each test, the vehicle's tax status, and basic vehicle details such as make, model, colour and fuel type.

Where does the data come from?

The results come from official DVSA MOT records, provided by IsItAGoodCar.com (by Matter Logic). The record covers cars, motorcycles and vans from 2005 onwards.

Why is there no MOT record for my car?

A vehicle under three years old has not had its first MOT yet, so no record exists. Some older vehicles are exempt, and Northern Ireland tests are recorded from 2017. Also check the registration for similar-looking characters, such as 0 and O.
